How to Use ChatGPT to Write Blog Posts That Rank on Google

By AI Side HustlesMarch 202611 min read
ChatGPT SEO Blog Posts That Rank

Most AI-generated blog content dies in obscurity. It gets written, published, and immediately buried under billions of other pages. The reason isn't that AI content is inherently bad — it's that most people use AI to generate content without understanding what Google actually rewards.

Google's algorithm is increasingly sophisticated. It doesn't just check for keywords — it evaluates E-E-A-T signals (Experience, Expertise, Authoritativeness, Trustworthiness), topical depth, user engagement signals, and structural quality. AI-generated content fails when it looks and feels generic, lacks genuine insight, and ignores the technical foundations of SEO.

Here's how to use ChatGPT to create blog posts that actually rank.

Step 1: Do Real Keyword Research First (Don't Skip This)

Before touching ChatGPT, you need a keyword worth targeting. The most common mistake is writing around overly competitive keywords with no chance of ranking. Use a process like this:

Find a broad seed topic ("AI side hustles"), use a tool like Ahrefs, Semrush, or Surfer SEO to identify keyword clusters — related questions and subtopics. Look for keywords with meaningful search volume (100–1,000+ monthly searches) but lower competition scores. Target question-based keywords and long-tail phrases where AI-generated content typically underperforms (because it lacks real answers).

Step 2: Research the Topic Before Writing

Feed ChatGPT real information to work with. Don't just ask it to "write a blog post about X." Instead:

  • Paste in key findings from 3–5 top-ranking articles on the topic
  • Ask: "What unique angles or perspectives do these articles miss?"
  • Ask: "What questions would a reader have after reading these that weren't answered?"
  • Use Perplexity or Google to gather current data, statistics, and examples

The quality of your output is determined by the quality of your input. Generic prompts produce generic content. Rich context produces valuable content.

Step 3: Build a Content Brief — Let ChatGPT Help (Carefully)

Create a detailed content brief that covers:

  • Primary keyword and 2–3 secondary keywords
  • Target word count based on what's ranking (typically 1,500–3,000 words for competitive topics)
  • Subheadings based on keyword research and competitor analysis
  • Questions to answer (from "People Also Ask" and Reddit threads)
  • Data points or examples to include

Use ChatGPT to draft the brief structure, but populate it with real data from real research.

Step 4: Write the First Draft in Sections

Don't generate the full article in one shot. Generate it section by section. For each section:

Provide the section heading, the target keyword, and specific points to cover. Ask for the section in a specific voice or style. After generation, immediately add your own expertise — a personal example, a counterintuitive take, a specific number from real research. This human layer is what separates ranking content from generic AI slop.

Step 5: Optimize for On-Page SEO

After the draft is complete, use ChatGPT (or Surfer SEO) to optimize it:

  • Check keyword density — target 1–2% naturally throughout
  • Add internal links to related content on your site
  • Include a FAQ section targeting featured snippet opportunities
  • Write a meta description that drives click-through (ChatGPT can draft 3–5 options)
  • Add schema markup where relevant (HowTo, FAQ, Article)
  • Use short paragraphs, bullet points, and clear headings for readability

The Common Mistakes That Kill Rankings

Publishing without editing: AI output always needs human editing. Read it aloud — if it sounds robotic, fix it.

Ignoring search intent: If top-ranking pages are all product reviews and you publish a informational article, you won't rank. Match the intent.

No unique angle: If your article says the same thing as the top 10 results, why would Google show yours? Bring a fresh perspective, real experience, or proprietary data.

Neglecting page experience: Site speed, mobile usability, and good UX signals directly impact rankings. Don't ignore the technical side.

The Bottom Line

ChatGPT is a powerful writing assistant — but it's not a magic ranking machine. The formula that works: genuine keyword research + real competitive analysis + AI-accelerated writing + substantial human editing and expertise = content that ranks.

Start with one article. Target a low-competition keyword. Execute every step above. Track your results. That one article will teach you more than any guide ever could.
